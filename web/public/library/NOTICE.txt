Chinese Reader starter library

The collection contains 34 texts: eighteen original modern practice readings and sixteen selections from ancient Chinese literature (twelve poems and four prose selections).

Ancient Chinese originals are public-domain works. Each classic includes its author, a direct Chinese Wikisource source URL, and any edition/excerpt note in the catalog and reader. Source pages were checked on 4 November 2026. This app uses simplified characters except for the selected Analects passage, and adapts punctuation and line breaks for practice. It does not reproduce modern commentary, published modern translations, or textbook layouts. English learning translations, modern practice texts, selection descriptions, and level labels were created for this app and are available under the repository's MIT license.

The catalog is web/src/data/catalog.ts and web/src/data/beginner.ts. Suggested phrase breaks are editable learning aids, not a unique or definitive performance of the text. The individual source links are authoritative for which version is represented. Quiet Night Thought follows the Tang Poems Three Hundred version; Sympathy for Farmers II preserves the source's 飧 rather than replacing it with 餐. Prose excerpts are marked as excerpts. The Analects selection omits the introductory 子曰 and quotation marks for practice.

Levels are suggested reading difficulty, not official HSK certification. Classical grammar and word meanings differ from modern Mandarin. Pinyin in the poems and selected classical lines is explicitly supplied for modern Mandarin learning; it does not reconstruct historical pronunciation or encode all spoken tone sandhi. Other pinyin and word meanings use the app's dictionary tools. No Mandarin teacher or literary translator has reviewed the complete collection.
